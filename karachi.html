Karachi – City of Lights

Karachi is Pakistan's largest city and its economic heartbeat. Situated along the Arabian Sea, it is a vibrant metropolis that blends colonial heritage with modern energy. From sun-drenched beaches to grand historical palaces, Karachi offers a rich tapestry of experiences for every visitor.

Clifton Beach

Clifton Beach is Karachi's most iconic seaside destination. Stretching along the Arabian Sea, it comes alive in the evenings with food stalls, carnival rides, and camel rides along the shore. The cool sea breeze and golden sunsets make it a favourite escape for families and tourists alike.

Mohatta Palace

Built in 1927, Mohatta Palace is a magnificent example of Indo-Saracenic architecture. Originally constructed as a summer residence, it now serves as a museum showcasing Pakistan's art, culture, and history. Its pink Jodhpur stone façade and ornate domes make it one of the most photographed landmarks in Karachi.

Pakistan Maritime Museum

The Pakistan Maritime Museum is a unique attraction dedicated to the naval history of Pakistan. Visitors can explore decommissioned warships, submarines, and aircraft displayed in an open-air setting. The museum provides a fascinating insight into Pakistan's maritime heritage and the strategic importance of Karachi as a port city.
