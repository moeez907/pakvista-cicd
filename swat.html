Swat – Switzerland of Pakistan

Swat Valley lies in the Khyber Pakhtunkhwa province and is one of Pakistan's most popular tourist destinations. Visitors come for its alpine scenery, skiing resorts, bazaars, and ancient Buddhist heritage.

Malam Jabba

Malam Jabba is Pakistan's premier ski resort, perched at about 2,804 metres above sea level. In winter it becomes a hub for skiing and snowboarding, while summer brings hiking trails through pine forests and panoramic views of the Hindu Kush range.

Mingora Bazaar

Mingora is the commercial heart of Swat Valley. Its bustling bazaar offers traditional handicrafts, embroidered shawls, honey, dried fruits, and local cuisine. Walking through the narrow lanes gives visitors a taste of Swati culture and hospitality.

Swat Museum

The Swat Museum in Mingora houses an impressive collection of Gandhara-era Buddhist artefacts, including sculptures, coins, and relics excavated from ancient sites across the valley. It is an essential stop for anyone interested in the region's history.
